This is the whole idea.

You waited until the end to talk about our course — and by then, Jack was ready to listen. That is the habit. Understand the person first. Find the feeling behind their words. Then show how we can help. When you offer the course too soon, the prospect may go quiet. When you ask more questions instead, they open up and tell you more.

Our course has not changed. What changed was you. You linked it to the real reason Jack came to see us: he wants to feel confident, and he wants to be respected.
